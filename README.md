## ✨ Features

- 🎨 **Color Palettes:** Choose from hand-picked gradients and sleek, modern colors designed to look premium on macOS.
- 🎭 **Icon Libraries:** Instantly browse, search, and apply icons from both **SF Symbols** and the extensive **Lucide Icons** collection.
- ⚡ **Performance:** Built 100% natively in Swift and SwiftUI for a blazing-fast, smooth, and lightweight experience.
- 🖥️ **Tailored UI:** Beautiful clean interface with dynamic dark mode support, fluid micro-animations, and glassmorphism details.

## 🚀 How to Download & Install

Fldery is ready to use without any complex setup.

1. Go to the **Releases** section on the right side of this page.
2. Download the latest **`Fldery.dmg`** file.
3. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Fldery** to your **Applications** folder.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **First Launch on macOS:**
> Since this app is signed ad-hoc, macOS will show a "Developer cannot be verified" warning at first launch. To open it:
> **Right-click (or Ctrl+click) Fldery in your Applications folder and select "Open"**, then confirm by clicking "Open" again in the pop-up window. This is only needed once!

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Openness
Fldery runs entirely locally on your Mac. It does not track you, does not connect to any servers, and requires zero network access. Your customization workflow is completely private.
